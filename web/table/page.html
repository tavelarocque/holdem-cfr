Every bot decision appears here: its equity against the ranges it puts on everyone still in the hand, the pot odds, and the probability it gave each action before sampling one. This is the bots' private reasoning, so it shows how strong their hands are.

Preflop, the bots rank hands by the exact all-in equity of all 169 starting hands, computed by holdem-cfr's strength tables (AA 85.20%, 72o 34.58%), and mix between actions near each threshold. After the flop they estimate equity by Monte Carlo against the ranges each opponent's actions imply, score each action with a rough expected value, and choose by regret matching: each action gets probability in proportion to how much better it looks than the average action. These are heuristic bots; the trained blueprint is a two-player strategy and plays in the heads-up mode.
